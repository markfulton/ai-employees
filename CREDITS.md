## Methods

The Sales Employee's booked call playbook (`employees/sales-employee/playbooks/booked-calls/`) adapts the inbound setter method Alex Hormozi and Acquisition.com teach publicly: reach the person soon after they book, offer the call now, ask three short questions, check the pre call video, and offer a same day time. The playbook is written in this project's own words, and its inclusion implies no endorsement in either direction.
