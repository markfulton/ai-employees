# Employee of the Week

One employee at a time, with a real output from a real run on my own business, scrubbed of contact names and nothing else. The plan is one a week starting the week after the release session, in this order: GTM Engineer, Chief of Staff, Sales Employee, Customer Satisfaction Employee, SEO Employee, Social Media Employee, Ad Manager Employee, Web Dev Employee.

Each entry carries the same five things: the routine that produced the output, the run record line, the output itself, what I corrected afterwards and where the correction went, and what it cost. Community entries from Discussions under Show and tell go here too, with your name on them.
