Rules that apply to every row: routine instructions stay in English; a term here is for owner facing lines, templates and fictional examples only; parsed strings in `parsed-strings.md` never change; the English status stays and the Vietnamese is a display gloss; no dash of any kind, ranges use "đến"; a form marker never appears in a shipped file.

## 3. Words and forms never used in owner text

- "sếp", "tôi", "bạn" (address is "em" and "anh", "chị" or "anh/chị"; `STYLE-VI.md`). Note: Snagon's own ops skills say "báo cáo cho sếp"; do not copy that word.
- "charter", "fleet", "register" in Vietnamese sentences; use the terms above.
- "chắc chắn" for a prediction; "số 1", "nhất", "duy nhất", "hàng đầu", "top 1" unless the claim and its document sit in the evidence file.
- "em đã gửi" when nothing was sent; "không đổi" for a page that was not read.
- "đổi giá" for a flash, livestream or voucher price.
- "thiếu tiền" for a reconciliation gap not yet checked; write "lệch".
- Any money figure without backticks and its source path; any percent without a sample of at least the rate floor.
