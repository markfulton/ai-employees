# social-media-employee-vn: glossary for owner facing Vietnamese

Every routine of this variant uses these forms, and only these, in text the member reads. Routine instructions stay in English; parsed strings stay in English (see `parsed-strings.md`). The shared vocabulary in `localization-reports/STYLE-VI.md` comes first and is repeated here only where this kit uses it. Role terms come from form Phần A, A4 (lines 108 to 135) and A3 (lines 95 to 106), filled by an AI and not yet reviewed by a practitioner (A1). A term is wording only: no rule enters a routine from this file.
